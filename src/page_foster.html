The Journey to a Forever Home Begins with a Foster Home.

For a German Shepherd, leaving the shelter isn't just a change of scenery — it can be the difference between surviving and truly living.

These loyal, sensitive dogs thrive on connection and routine. Torn from what's familiar, many become frightened and overwhelmed in a kennel — behaviors that reflect the trauma, not who the dog really is. A foster home gives them the quiet space to heal, and gives us the time to learn who they truly are before we match them with the family they'll love for life.

What Southern Cross Provides

You provide the love. We'll help with the rest.

Opening your home to a foster dog is an incredible gift — but you won't be doing it alone. Depending on each dog's needs, we provide veterinary care, food and supplies, crates and equipment, behavioral guidance, and ongoing support from experienced volunteers.

Our goal is simple: give you the tools and support you need to focus on what matters most — helping a German Shepherd feel safe, loved, and ready for their forever home.

Can I adopt the dog I'm fostering?

Yes — this is called foster-to-adopt. If you're not sure you're ready for a lifetime commitment, fostering lets you open your home first with no pressure. If it turns out to be the right fit, adoption is always an option. There's no wrong outcome: whether you foster for two weeks or become a forever home, you're changing a dog's life.

You May Already Be Exactly What a Dog Needs

You don't need acres of land or a training certificate.

One of the biggest misconceptions about fostering is that you need to be an experienced trainer or completely rearrange your life. You don't. What German Shepherds need most is something many people already have: a safe home, a little patience, and someone willing to care.

Our team carefully matches dogs with foster families based on experience and lifestyle — whether it's your first time fostering or your fifth, you'll never do it alone. If you have room in your home and room in your heart, you may already have everything it takes to change a dog's life.
