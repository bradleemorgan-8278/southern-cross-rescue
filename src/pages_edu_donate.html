Southern Cross believes informed ownership creates better outcomes for both dogs and families.

Breed Advocacy

Backyard Breeding Harms the Breed

Irresponsible breeding continues to create major challenges for German Shepherd rescue organizations nationwide.

Breeding for appearance without regard for temperament, health, working traits, or long-term welfare has lasting consequences for dogs and owners alike.

We advocate for responsible stewardship of the breed and informed ownership decisions.
