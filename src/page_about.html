Our Story

A Mission Sustained by Stewardship

[ Photo — Molly Moncure and/or Adrienne Donahue: a single portrait, or the two together ]

Southern Cross German Shepherd Rescue was founded by Molly Moncure, whose deep respect for the intelligence, loyalty, and working heritage of the German Shepherd inspired a rescue dedicated to giving these remarkable dogs the second chances they deserve.

When Molly became seriously ill and the future of the rescue was uncertain, longtime volunteer Adrienne Donahue stepped forward to ensure the mission would continue. What began as an act of service became a lifelong commitment to preserving a rescue that so many dogs had come to depend on.

Adrienne was never alone in that commitment. From the earliest days, dedicated team members Janice, Dana, and Laura — along with the unwavering support of countless foster families, volunteers, adopters, donors, veterinarians, and transporters — helped Southern Cross grow into one of Georgia's most respected breed-specific rescues.

Today, Southern Cross proudly honors Molly Moncure for the vision that began this journey, and Adrienne Donahue for the extraordinary stewardship that ensured it would endure.

Our Mission

Rescue. Rehabilitate. Rehome.

Southern Cross German Shepherd Rescue is dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming German Shepherds throughout Georgia through compassionate foster-based care, responsible advocacy, and community education.

What We Believe

Six Commitments to the Breed

Every Shepherd Deserves the Chance to Thrive

They deserve safety, understanding, and the opportunity to become the loyal companions they were born to be.

Rescue Is Only the Beginning

It's the first step in helping them heal, rebuild trust, and rediscover who they truly are.

Foster Homes Change Lives

Foster care provides the stability and patience that lets frightened dogs decompress and regain confidence.

Thoughtful Matches Create Lifelong Families

We measure success by how confidently a dog begins their next chapter — not how quickly they're adopted.

Education Strengthens the Breed

Through responsible ownership and breed advocacy, we work to prevent future surrenders.

Stewardship Is Our Promise

Every dog entrusted to us deserves transparency, accountability, and their well-being at the center of every decision.
