About Southern Cross
German Shepherd Rescue

Southern Cross German Shepherd Rescue wasn't born from a business plan — it was born from a deep respect for one of the world's most devoted and misunderstood breeds.

What began as a small, volunteer-driven rescue has grown into a mission dedicated to giving German Shepherds the second chance they deserve. Built on compassion, knowledge, and an unwavering commitment to the breed, Southern Cross exists for the dogs who have nowhere else to turn.

We believe German Shepherds are unlike any other breed. They form extraordinary bonds with their people and thrive on loyalty, purpose, and connection. When those bonds are broken through abandonment, surrender, neglect, or loss, they don't stop loving — they simply wait for someone new to love.

That's why our work is about more than rescue.

Through foster care, medical rehabilitation, thoughtful evaluation, responsible placement, and community education, we help each dog leave the fear of the shelter behind, rediscover who they are, and find the family where they will thrive for a lifetime.

Because every German Shepherd deserves more than a second chance.
They deserve the chance to belong.

Story

Our Story

A Mission Sustained by Stewardship

Southern Cross German Shepherd Rescue was founded by Molly Moncure, whose deep respect for the intelligence, loyalty, and working heritage of the German Shepherd inspired a rescue dedicated to giving these remarkable dogs the second chances they deserve.

When Molly became seriously ill and the future of the rescue was uncertain, longtime volunteer Adrienne Donahue stepped forward to ensure the mission would continue. What began as an act of service became a lifelong commitment to preserving a rescue that so many dogs had come to depend on.

Adrienne was never alone in that commitment. From the earliest days, dedicated team members Janice, Dana, and Laura, along with the unwavering support of countless foster families, volunteers, adopters, donors, veterinarians, transporters, and supporters, helped Southern Cross grow from a small volunteer effort into one of Georgia's most respected breed-specific rescues.

Together, they built far more than an organization. They built a community united by a shared belief that every German Shepherd deserves compassion, understanding, and the opportunity to thrive.

For more than two decades, that community has provided hope for dogs facing abandonment, neglect, medical hardship, and uncertain futures. Every rescue, every foster home, every adoption, and every success story reflects the collective commitment of people who refused to let the mission fail.

Today, Southern Cross proudly honors Molly Moncure for the vision that began this journey and Adrienne Donahue for the extraordinary stewardship that ensured it would endure. Their leadership laid the foundation upon which generations of volunteers have continued to build.

As Southern Cross enters its next chapter, we remain committed to preserving that legacy while preparing for the future. Through thoughtful governance, sustainable growth, operational excellence, and responsible stewardship, we are building an organization that will continue serving German Shepherds — and the people who love them — for generations to come.

Because the mission was never about one person.
It has always been about ensuring that every German Shepherd has the opportunity to find safety, healing, and the place where they belong.

What We Believe

Six Commitments to the Breed

01

Every German Shepherd Deserves the Chance to Thrive

We believe every German Shepherd deserves more than survival. They deserve safety, understanding, and the opportunity to become the loyal, devoted companions they were born to be.

02

Rescue Is Only the Beginning

Rescue isn't simply removing a dog from a shelter. It's the first step in helping them heal, rebuild trust, and begin a new chapter. We believe every dog deserves the time and care to rediscover who they truly are.

03

Foster Homes Change Lives

German Shepherds thrive in homes, not kennels. Foster care provides the stability, patience, and love that allows frightened dogs to decompress, regain confidence, and reveal the remarkable companions they have always been.

04

Thoughtful Matches Create Lifelong Families

We don't measure success by how quickly a dog is adopted. We measure it by how confidently they begin their next chapter. By understanding each dog's personality, needs, and temperament, we create lasting matches that benefit both the dog and the family.

05

Education Strengthens the Breed

Many dogs enter rescue because families simply weren't prepared for the unique needs of this extraordinary breed. Through education, responsible ownership, and breed advocacy, we hope to prevent future surrenders and strengthen the bond between German Shepherds and the people who love them.

06

Stewardship Is Our Promise

Every dog entrusted to Southern Cross deserves compassionate care, ethical decision-making, and responsible stewardship. We are committed to transparency, accountability, and always placing the well-being of our dogs at the center of every decision we make.
