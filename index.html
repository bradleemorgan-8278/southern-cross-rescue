Mission

Our Mission

More Than Rescue

At Southern Cross German Shepherd Rescue, we believe every German Shepherd deserves more than survival — they deserve the opportunity to thrive.

For generations, these extraordinary dogs have faithfully served mankind as protectors, police and military partners, service dogs, search and rescue heroes, loyal companions, and beloved members of countless families. They give everything they have to the people they love.

Yet too many find themselves abandoned, surrendered, neglected, or misunderstood. And for a breed that craves connection, purpose, and belonging, the shelter environment can be especially devastating.

That's why we do more than rescue dogs.

We move them from uncertainty to safety, from shelter to foster, and from foster to forever. Along the way, we provide medical care, rehabilitation, thoughtful evaluation, and the time they need to heal and reveal the remarkable companions they were always meant to be.

Through foster-based rescue, responsible placement, education, and breed advocacy, we're not simply saving lives — we're building a future where more German Shepherds have the opportunity to live the lives they deserve.

"We move them from uncertainty to safety, from shelter to foster, and from foster to forever."

Why Fostering Matters

Every Foster Home Saves More Than One Life

German Shepherds are extraordinary dogs — but they are among the breeds that struggle most in a shelter environment.

They are deeply loyal, highly intelligent, and form powerful bonds with their people. When they suddenly find themselves alone in a noisy kennel, surrounded by unfamiliar sights, sounds, and smells, many become frightened, anxious, and confused.

What some people mistake as aggression is often fear, stress, and heartbreak.

The longer a German Shepherd remains in a shelter, the greater the risk that this overwhelming stress begins to change their behavior. Dogs that once lived happily in a home can become withdrawn, reactive, or emotionally shut down — not because they are dangerous, but because they are failing to thrive in an environment they were never meant to endure.

That's why foster homes are so vital.

A foster home gives a dog the opportunity to decompress, feel safe again, and begin trusting people once more. It allows us to truly understand their personality, provide any needed medical care or training, and match them with the family that is right for them — not simply the first family that comes along.

Every open foster home creates space for another dog to leave the shelter before trauma has the chance to define them.

You don't have to adopt to save a life.

Whether you foster for two weeks or two months, you become the bridge between uncertainty and a forever home. Southern Cross provides veterinary care, guidance, supplies, and ongoing support throughout the journey.

Because every German Shepherd deserves the chance to be known for who they truly are — not for how they survived the shelter.

"A shelter shows us a dog's survival instincts. A foster home shows us their heart."

Who We Are

About Southern Cross
German Shepherd Rescue

Southern Cross German Shepherd Rescue wasn't born from a business plan — it was born from a deep respect for one of the world's most devoted and misunderstood breeds.

What began as a small, volunteer-driven rescue has grown into a mission dedicated to giving German Shepherds the second chance they deserve. Built on compassion, knowledge, and an unwavering commitment to the breed, Southern Cross exists for the dogs who have nowhere else to turn.

We believe German Shepherds are unlike any other breed. They form extraordinary bonds with their people and thrive on loyalty, purpose, and connection. When those bonds are broken through abandonment, surrender, neglect, or loss, they don't stop loving — they simply wait for someone new to love.

That's why our work is about more than rescue.

Through foster care, medical rehabilitation, thoughtful evaluation, responsible placement, and community education, we help each dog leave the fear of the shelter behind, rediscover who they are, and find the family where they will thrive for a lifetime.

Because every German Shepherd deserves more than a second chance.
They deserve the chance to belong.

Story

Our Story

A Mission Sustained by Stewardship

Southern Cross German Shepherd Rescue was founded by Molly Moncure, whose deep respect for the intelligence, loyalty, and working heritage of the German Shepherd inspired a rescue dedicated to giving these remarkable dogs the second chances they deserve.

When Molly became seriously ill and the future of the rescue was uncertain, longtime volunteer Adrienne Donahue stepped forward to ensure the mission would continue. What began as an act of service became a lifelong commitment to preserving a rescue that so many dogs had come to depend on.

Adrienne was never alone in that commitment. From the earliest days, dedicated team members Janice, Dana, and Laura, along with the unwavering support of countless foster families, volunteers, adopters, donors, veterinarians, transporters, and supporters, helped Southern Cross grow from a small volunteer effort into one of Georgia's most respected breed-specific rescues.

Together, they built far more than an organization. They built a community united by a shared belief that every German Shepherd deserves compassion, understanding, and the opportunity to thrive.

For more than two decades, that community has provided hope for dogs facing abandonment, neglect, medical hardship, and uncertain futures. Every rescue, every foster home, every adoption, and every success story reflects the collective commitment of people who refused to let the mission fail.

Today, Southern Cross proudly honors Molly Moncure for the vision that began this journey and Adrienne Donahue for the extraordinary stewardship that ensured it would endure. Their leadership laid the foundation upon which generations of volunteers have continued to build.

As Southern Cross enters its next chapter, we remain committed to preserving that legacy while preparing for the future. Through thoughtful governance, sustainable growth, operational excellence, and responsible stewardship, we are building an organization that will continue serving German Shepherds — and the people who love them — for generations to come.

Because the mission was never about one person.
It has always been about ensuring that every German Shepherd has the opportunity to find safety, healing, and the place where they belong.

What We Believe

Six Commitments to the Breed

01

Every German Shepherd Deserves the Chance to Thrive

We believe every German Shepherd deserves more than survival. They deserve safety, understanding, and the opportunity to become the loyal, devoted companions they were born to be.

02

Rescue Is Only the Beginning

Rescue isn't simply removing a dog from a shelter. It's the first step in helping them heal, rebuild trust, and begin a new chapter. We believe every dog deserves the time and care to rediscover who they truly are.

03

Foster Homes Change Lives

German Shepherds thrive in homes, not kennels. Foster care provides the stability, patience, and love that allows frightened dogs to decompress, regain confidence, and reveal the remarkable companions they have always been.

04

Thoughtful Matches Create Lifelong Families

We don't measure success by how quickly a dog is adopted. We measure it by how confidently they begin their next chapter. By understanding each dog's personality, needs, and temperament, we create lasting matches that benefit both the dog and the family.

05

Education Strengthens the Breed

Many dogs enter rescue because families simply weren't prepared for the unique needs of this extraordinary breed. Through education, responsible ownership, and breed advocacy, we hope to prevent future surrenders and strengthen the bond between German Shepherds and the people who love them.

06

Stewardship Is Our Promise

Every dog entrusted to Southern Cross deserves compassionate care, ethical decision-making, and responsible stewardship. We are committed to transparency, accountability, and always placing the well-being of our dogs at the center of every decision we make.

Open Your Home

Foster a German Shepherd

The Journey to a Forever Home Begins with a Foster Home.

For a German Shepherd, leaving the shelter isn't just a change of scenery — it can be the difference between surviving and truly living.

These remarkable dogs are deeply loyal and thrive on connection, routine, and purpose. When they're suddenly separated from the people they know and placed in the unfamiliar, stressful environment of a shelter, many become frightened, anxious, and overwhelmed. The behaviors people see in a kennel are often not a reflection of who the dog truly is, but of the trauma they're experiencing.

A foster home changes that.

It provides the quiet place where fear begins to fade, trust begins to return, and a dog's true personality has the chance to shine. It's where they can rest, heal, learn, and prepare for the family they've been waiting for.

Just as importantly, fostering gives our rescue the time to truly know each dog. Instead of making life-changing decisions based on how a dog copes in a shelter, we get to see how they interact in a home, what brings them joy, what kind of family will help them thrive, and where they will be happiest for years to come.

Every foster home opens the door for another German Shepherd still waiting in a shelter.

When you foster through Southern Cross German Shepherd Rescue, you are never doing it alone. We provide veterinary care, guidance, supplies when needed, and a community of experienced volunteers who will support you every step of the way.

You don't have to make a lifelong commitment to change a life.

Sometimes, the greatest gift you can give is helping a dog find the family where they belong forever.

We don't rush dogs into homes — we prepare them for the right home.

We believe every German Shepherd deserves more than a quick placement. By allowing dogs to decompress in foster care, we learn who they truly are, helping us make thoughtful, lasting matches that benefit both the dog and the family. A successful adoption isn't measured by how quickly a dog leaves our care, but by how confidently they begin their next chapter.

You May Already Be Exactly What a Dog Needs

One of the biggest misconceptions about fostering is that you have to be an experienced dog trainer, have acres of land, or completely rearrange your life.

You don't.

What German Shepherds need most is something many people already have — a safe home, a little patience, and someone willing to care.

Our team carefully matches dogs with foster families based on experience, lifestyle, and the individual needs of each dog. Whether you're fostering for the first time or have years of experience, you'll never be expected to do it alone.

If you have room in your home and room in your heart, you may already have everything it takes to change a dog's life.

We will be with you every step of the journey.

"You don't have to change every dog's life. Just one. We'll help with the rest."

Philosophy

Our Adoption Philosophy

German Shepherds are extraordinary companions. They are intelligent, deeply loyal, and eager to form lasting bonds with the people they trust. They thrive in homes that provide structure, consistency, meaningful engagement, training, and patient guidance.

At Southern Cross, we believe successful adoptions begin long before a dog goes home.

Each dog spends time in foster care where they have the opportunity to decompress, heal, and reveal their true personality. Our foster families help us understand not only a dog's temperament and needs, but also the type of home where they will flourish.

We believe the best adoptions aren't the fastest — they're the ones that last.

That's why we are committed to honest communication, thoughtful matching, and finding the right family for every dog in our care. When we make the right match, everyone benefits: the dog, the adopter, and the lifelong bond they build together.

Adoption Process

Seven Steps to Forever

Select any step to see what to expect.

Start by telling us about your home, your household, and the kind of companion you're hoping to welcome. The application helps us understand your lifestyle so we can begin thinking about which dogs might be the right fit.

Our team reviews every application personally. This isn't about finding reasons to say no — it's about understanding your situation well enough to make a match that lasts a lifetime.

We confirm that any current pets are well cared for and speak with your references. These conversations help us picture the daily life a dog would be joining.

You'll meet the dog (or dogs) we believe could be a strong match, along with the foster who knows them best. This is where connection begins and questions get answered honestly.

A brief home visit helps us confirm the space is safe and ready — and gives us a chance to offer setup tips that make those first days smoother for both of you.

Once everyone agrees it's the right match, we finalize the adoption and welcome you officially into the Southern Cross family.

Our relationship doesn't end at adoption. We remain a resource for guidance, training questions, and support for the entire life of your dog.

Breed Advocacy

Backyard Breeding Harms the Breed

Irresponsible breeding continues to create major challenges for German Shepherd rescue organizations nationwide.

Breeding for appearance without regard for temperament, health, working traits, or long-term welfare has lasting consequences for dogs and owners alike.

We advocate for responsible stewardship of the breed and informed ownership decisions.
